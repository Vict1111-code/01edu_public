## How to study and validate

1. Work through the stages in order, but regard within-stage order as flexible until requirements and prerequisites are reviewed.
2. Open the official subject README for requirements and examples; follow test links where available.
3. Implement independently and test edge cases; do not copy the community solutions.
4. Check actual quest availability, timing, and audit requirements in your authorized Galaxy student interface.
5. Track concepts mastered separately from exercise completion.

This document preserves the learner archive's original folder names only as provenance. It is not a claim about official Galaxy scheduling.
